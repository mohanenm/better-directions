      Walk length

        Better Directions finds a deliberately longer walk between two places, and shows the normal route next to it so you know what the detour costs.

        It only uses paths that are open to walkers in OpenStreetMap. It never routes along railways, motorways, construction or anything marked private, and it only goes through buildings by mapped public passages. It can still be wrong: the map may be out of date, and gates and doors close. Follow signs, and skip anything that looks closed or unsafe.

        Everything runs on your device. There are no cookies, no tracking and no accounts, and the page talks to no one but this site. Your location is used only when you tap "Use my location", and it stays on your device. Shared links and images include your start and end points.
